2. What PRIVÉ is

A concierge writes a storyline for you. Each of its scenes is a spoken conversation with a character; a coach reads it and tells you what worked and the one thing to change; you play the scene again.

  • The characters, the concierge and the coach are generated by language models, and their voices are synthesised. No person is on the other side of a scene.
  • What they say is written for you by a machine and can be wrong: a line, a correction or a note may be mistaken or unnatural.
  • PRIVÉ is practice. It is not a language certificate, and a scene that rehearses a medical, legal, financial or official situation is not advice on that matter.
