Privacy Policy

PRIVÉ is a private tutor, and a tutor has to remember you to be of any use. This page says what we keep, why, who handles it for us, and how you take it away.

Last updated 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

PRIVÉ is operated by Wisecraft Ltd, a company established in Cyprus ("we"). We decide how the personal data described here is used, which makes us its controller. Write to concierge@prive.talk about anything on this page.

What the tutor knows about you comes only from what you said. We do not record facts about your health, religion, politics, sexuality, money or dealings with the law there, even when a storyline rehearses such a situation. In the app, under Your storylines, What you now do, you can read what is known about you and remove any line: a line you remove is never written again.

3. Your voice

When you speak, your recording is sent to our server so that it can be heard: by a Google Gemini model during a scene, and by Microsoft Azure Speech together with a Gemini model when a rehearsal or warm-up line is checked. We keep the words, not the sound: a recording is not stored on our servers once it has been processed.

On your phone, a recording stays in the app's temporary storage so that you can hear yourself, and is cleared by the app.

The voices you hear are synthesised. Short clips of them are kept in a cache for up to thirty days so that a line plays at once.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Nothing you say shapes another learner's experience. Your storylines, your scenes and your notes are written for you and shown to no one else.
  • We do not use your conversations to train models of our own.
  • The app makes no decision about you that has a legal effect or a similarly significant one.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account, storylines, scenes, notes and what the tutor knows about you: for as long as you have an account.
  • Deleting your account, in the app under Settings, Account, removes all of it and your sign-in at once. It cannot be undone. The caches empty on their own within forty-five days.
  • Diagnostics: for a limited period set by Sentry, after which they are deleted.
  • Records of your purchases are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat under their own policies.

7. Your choices and your rights

  • Read it and correct it. What you now do shows what the tutor knows about you; a line that is not true goes at a tap. Your name and your other settings are changed in Settings.
  • Take it with you. Settings, Account, Take it with you gives you everything we keep about you as one file.
  • Delete it. Settings, Account, Delete your account.
  • The microphone. You can withdraw the app's access to it in your phone's settings at any time, and write your lines instead.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you also have the right to ask us for access to your data, to have it corrected or erased, to have its use restricted, to object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Write to concierge@prive.talk; we answer within one month. You may also complain to the data protection authority of the country you live in, or to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

8. The grounds we rely on

  • Our contract with you, for everything needed to provide the service: your account, your storylines, scenes and notes, what the tutor knows about you, and your membership's capacity.
  • Our legitimate interest in a service that works and is not abused, for diagnostics and security.
  • Your consent, for the microphone.
  • The law, where it requires us to keep something.

9. Children

PRIVÉ is not meant for anyone under sixteen, and we do not knowingly keep data about them. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your data travels encrypted between your phone and our servers, and our database encrypts it where it is stored. Within Wisecraft Ltd, access to it is limited to what is needed to run and repair the service.

11. Changes to this page

When we change this policy we change the date at its top. If a change matters to how your data is used, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
